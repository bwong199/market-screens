ETFs: Trending ETF universe (AUM at least $20M / C$20M, long-only, no bond funds, no leveraged, inverse or single-stock funds, 10D average volume at least 6,500). Stocks: every name in the Trending stock universe (US, TSX, TSXV) with 10D average volume at least 6,500, market cap at least $1B (US) / C$300M (Canada). AUM and market cap are in the fund's or stock's listing currency (C$ for Canadian listings). Prices and market cap from Yahoo; the as-of date is the last complete session. A screen, not advice.
