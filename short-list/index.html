The 250D screen lists names below the lowest daily low of the 250 sessions before the latest close. The 150D screen uses 150 sessions and includes every 200D name. Canadian prices are in CAD; market cap is converted to USD. For ETFs the last column shows fund AUM (Yahoo Finance total assets).
